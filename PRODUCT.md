# Omertà — product

## Who it is for
A friend group playing Mafia in person, usually 6–16 people around a table or a floor, phones in hand.
One of them is the Game Master. They are not a professional moderator: they want the night to run itself,
the rulings to be right, and the reveals to land. Players are mostly in their late teens and twenties.

## The job
- **Game Master:** set up a fair game in a minute, run every night without a rulebook open, never leak a role,
  and deliver deaths with some theatre. Pick up a saved setup or a half-finished game on any device.
- **Player:** see my role privately, make my night move without being seen, get my private results, watch the
  morning land, vote. If I'm Mafia, talk to my team without the table hearing.

## Principles
1. **The table is the stage.** The phone supports the room; it never asks people to stare at it during the day.
2. **Nothing leaks.** No colour, sound, vibration, toast or refusal message may hint at a hidden role. Every phone
   looks the same at night whatever the role. Errors shown to a phone never name another player's role.
3. **One thing per screen.** Each phase has one primary action. Secondary actions sit quietly underneath.
4. **Rulings by the book, overrides by hand.** The engine resolves; the Game Master can always undo or change it.
5. **Works on a bad connection.** Online play degrades to polling; the Game Master's phone never needs the network.

## Tone
Plain, short, a little dramatic at the moments that matter (night falls, a name flatlines, the winner's light).
Never cute, never jargon. Second person for players ("Your move."), imperative for the Game Master.

## Out of scope
Matchmaking with strangers, voice, accounts for players, public lobbies.
